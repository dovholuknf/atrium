Push-to-talk dictation, mock. Hold Right Ctrl with the terminal focused (or hold the mic in the bar) to talk, release to transcribe. The words are pasted into the input line followed by a new line that does not submit. You press Enter when it reads right. Esc while holding throws the clip away. On the phone, hold the mic beside Send. Release appends the words and a new line to the box, and Send submits. Audio goes only to this room's whisper on sg4's GPU (about 0.4 s for a 5 s clip, 2 s for 35 s). Try it here: the key and both mic buttons are live with canned text.
