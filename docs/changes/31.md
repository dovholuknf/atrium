# 31: STUCK no longer fires while a worker waits on background runs

Design: `31-design.md`.

## Changelog

- A worker whose turn ends with background shells or headless runs still going is no longer marked STUCK, and its
  launcher is not told it stopped silently. `atrium turn` now sends `background_running`, the count of running
  non-subagent entries in the Stop payload's `background_tasks`, and the daemon holds the silent-stop alert while it
  is above zero. When the work finishes the session wakes and stops again, and that later turn end is the clock.
  The hold is capped at two hours (`ATRIUM_A2A_BACKGROUND_HOLD`) so a dev server left up cannot hide a real stop.

## Test plan

Manual, on an agent-launched worker under a room with the new binary and its Stop hook installed:

1. Launch a worker and tell it to start three `run_in_background` shells that each sleep four minutes, then end its
   turn without calling `atrium_report`.
2. Watch the card for five minutes. It sits in needs-input, shows no STUCK, and the launcher receives no
   "ended its turn without reporting" notice.
3. When the shells finish the worker wakes, reads them, and stops again. If it still says nothing to the launcher,
   the notice and STUCK arrive two minutes after that second stop, not after the first.
4. Repeat with no background work: STUCK and the notice arrive as before.
