## Test plan

## @LETTER@. Fold the cards a parent launched

### @LETTER@1. Fold and unfold

In the terminal list, press the chevron on a parent row that has spawned cards. The running children hide and the chevron shows how many. Press it again and they return. Tab to the chevron and press Enter or Space to do the same.

### @LETTER@2. A question stays visible

Fold a parent while one child waits on a permission. That child stays drawn under the folded row.

### @LETTER@3. Remembered and shared

Reload the board, and open a second window. Both keep the fold. Fold or unfold in one window and the other follows.
