## Test plan

## @LETTER@. Automatic new context on a tagged card

### @LETTER@1. A tagged card is cycled at a low line

1. In settings set `auto_new_context` to `tagged` and `auto_new_context_k` to `50` (lower `context_threshold_k` to `50` first, since the line may not sit under it).
2. Tag one live Claude card `atrium:auto-new-context` and work it past that size, then leave it idle at its prompt with no browser attached for ten minutes (45 seconds for an agent card).

**Expected:** its chip reads as automatic, it writes its handoff, clears, and wakes from the handoff in the same card. The card's history shows a started and a done entry by `auto-new-context`. It does not cycle again until it has shrunk and grown past the line.

### @LETTER@2. Untagged cards and the off setting are left alone

1. Leave a second card untagged at the same size. Then set `auto_new_context` to `off`.

**Expected:** the untagged card is never cycled under `tagged`, and a tagged card past the line stays as it is under `off`.
