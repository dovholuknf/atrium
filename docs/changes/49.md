## Test plan

## @LETTER@. A card on every room

### @LETTER@1. Say to the orchestrator by name from another room

1. On the orchestrator's room, tag its card `atrium:everywhere`.
2. From a card on another room, `atrium_say to=orchestrator`.

**Expected:** it delivers, and the answer's `to` is the orchestrator's `handle@room`.

### @LETTER@2. A local card wins

1. On the other room, alias a card `orchestrator`.
2. Say to `orchestrator` from a card on that room, then from a card on a third room.

**Expected:** the first reaches the local card. The third still reaches the everywhere card.

### @LETTER@3. Two rooms, one name

1. Tag a card aliased `orchestrator` on two rooms. Say to `orchestrator` from a third room.

**Expected:** refused, naming `handle@room (@orchestrator)` for both, and nothing delivered.

### @LETTER@4. The owning room is not attached

1. Stop the orchestrator's room. Say to `orchestrator` from another room.

**Expected:** the answer is `held`, and it arrives when the room reattaches.

### @LETTER@5. The hub is stopped

1. Stop the hub. Say to `orchestrator` from a room.

**Expected:** a 404 that says the hub is not answering, and nothing is held.

### @LETTER@6. Exit does not fall through

1. `atrium_exit orchestrator` from another room, then `atrium_exit orchestrator@<room>`.

**Expected:** the first is a local 404. The second asks the orchestrator to leave.

### @LETTER@7. The scoped view

1. Open another room's scoped board with a build that draws everywhere cards.
2. Move the orchestrator's column, then untag it.

**Expected:** the card shows with a room chip and moves live, a permission on it shows no answer buttons, and untagging
drops it within a few seconds without a reload. (Needs the board part, which is not in this item.)

### @LETTER@8. An old board sees no change

1. Open `/v1/events/room/<name>` with no parameter while the orchestrator is tagged.

**Expected:** only that room's own events, and no `everywhere` event.

### @LETTER@9. A new room against an old hub

1. Run `atrium_peers` without `rooms` on a room whose hub predates this item.

**Expected:** the local rows only.
