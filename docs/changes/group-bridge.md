## Test plan

## @LETTER@. A shut group carries the bridge

### @LETTER@1. Attach into a shut group

1. On the Terminals tab, shut the group that holds a session, then attach that session from the switcher.

**Expected:** the group stays shut. The bridge runs across the gutter from the group's heading to the pane, and the
heading wears the same frame the attached row wears.

### @LETTER@2. Open and shut the group

1. Open the group.
2. Shut it again.

**Expected:** opening moves the bridge to the card's row, and shutting moves it back to the heading. With nested
groups the outermost shut one carries it. Scroll the heading out of view and the bridge goes with it.
