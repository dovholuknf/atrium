## Changelog

- **A resident director stops ringing its launcher while its workers are out.** See `docs/backlog-2.md` r-007.

  The "ended its turn without reporting" notice and the board's STUCK mark both come from `stoppedSilently`. A card
  tagged `atrium:director` is now not silently stopped while any card whose work item names it as launcher still has
  a live runner, in any status, a `done` worker at its prompt included. A culled or dead worker does not count. Once
  every worker has ended the director's silent stop works as before, one notice on the usual backoff. A worker's own
  silent stop is unchanged.

## Test plan

## @LETTER@. A director is quiet while its workers are out

### @LETTER@1. Director with a live worker

1. Launch a director tagged `atrium:director` from the orchestrator, and have it launch a worker.
2. Let the director end a turn without reporting to the orchestrator.

**Expected:** no "ended its turn without reporting" notice reaches the orchestrator and the director shows no STUCK
mark.

### @LETTER@2. Every worker ended

1. Cull the worker, or let it exit.
2. Let the director end another turn without reporting.

**Expected:** the orchestrator gets exactly one notice, and the director shows STUCK on the usual backoff.
