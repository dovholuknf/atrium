## Test plan

## @LETTER@. A pane shorter than the pty draws all of it and clips to the cursor (t-003c)

### @LETTER@1. A short pane, a tall pty
Needs a room where the pty is taller than this pane, which the daemon half of t-003 makes happen (on a room without it, this cannot be seen and nothing changes). Open a card in a short pane while a taller window is attached. The terminal draws the pty's full height, the pane clips it, and the prompt stays in view while you type. There is no second scrollbar: only xterm's own on the right.

### @LETTER@2. What stays in view
With Claude Code idle, the input box, its border, the status line and the mode line under the prompt are all visible together. Make the pane very short: the prompt row and the one under it win over the rest.

### @LETTER@3. Wheel, focus, links, mouse
Wheel up: the rows the clip hid come into view and the clip does not fight you. Click into the terminal and type: the pane does not jump. A URL in the visible part opens on a click. With an app that reports the mouse, a click lands on the cell under the pointer. Find bar and hover tips stay inside the pane.

### @LETTER@4. Resize and pop-out
Drag a split or resize a popped-out window: the clip and fit rerun, and the resize this window sends says what it can show, never the pty's height. Make the pane as tall as the pty: the clip goes away and the grid sits on the footer as before.

### @LETTER@5. Known limit
A drag selection past the pane's bottom does not autoscroll the clipped rows (xterm scrolls its own viewport, and this pane is not it). Scroll with the wheel and continue. The phone is unchanged: it never clips and never sends a resize.

### @LETTER@6. The console prefix
With input lag logging on, every board console line reads `[atrium inputlag]`, so a console filter of `[atrium` keeps them.
