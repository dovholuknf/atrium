## Test plan

## @LETTER@. The rooms dashboard: cpu and memory sparklines, and words instead of dashes

How the machine band tells its states apart, from the snapshot alone:

- No `machine` object: the room's build does not send it. Says "cpu and memory: this room's build does not report them".
- A `machine` object with neither cpu nor memory: no sample yet. Says "cpu and memory: waiting for the first reading".
- Memory present and `cpu_pct` absent: CPU is unavailable on that room (macOS has no machine CPU without cgo). Says "cpu: not available on this room". The one exception is a room whose process started under 30 seconds ago, where the second sample is still due: "cpu: waiting for the second reading".
- `stale_since` set: the figures stay, dimmed, with "stale since HH:MM". A stale room is left out of busiest and tightest, and does not pulse for a hot CPU.
- A `null` in a series is a gap in the line and never a zero.

### @LETTER@1. Both sparklines
Open the room menu on a board with `?demo=rooms`. sg3 and sg4 each show `cpu NN%` with a blue line and `mem NN%` with a purple line and `used/total GB used` under it. The memory colour is `--spark-mem`, which a skin may set.

### @LETTER@2. Words, not dashes
m1mini in the demo sends no `machine`: its machine band reads that the room's build does not report cpu and memory. No dash appears in cpu or mem in any room.

### @LETTER@3. Gaps and stale
A room whose series starts with nulls draws its line starting where the samples do, with a break for a null in the middle. A snapshot with `stale_since` shows "stale since HH:MM" and dimmed figures.

### @LETTER@4. The all-rooms tile
The rooms band has at most three rows, in this order: live and away, "N not the hub's build" (only when a room is off it, and only when the hub's build is known), "busiest sg3 62%", "tightest sg4 86% mem". The last one gives way when the build warning is drawn. Rows with nothing to say are not drawn, and no row shows a dash.

### @LETTER@5. Phone
At 390x844 the tiles stack, the sparklines stay, and the panel is no wider than the window.
