# u-020: the phone terminal bar is one slim row

## Changelog

- On a phone (`body.term-phone`), including the popped-out `#term=` window, the terminal pane's own header is collapsed by default to one slim row: the alias (or title), a chevron and full screen. The chevron (a 44px button with aria-label and aria-expanded) or a tap on the bare row expands it to the mark, chips (room, pid, path), agent/shell, ctrl-c, pop out, files, exit and the cog. The choice is one saved per-device setting shared with the board header (`atrium.phone.headerOpen`, body class `hdr-open`, `phoneHeaderOpenApply`/`phoneHeaderOpenSet` in termfull.js), and a `storage` listener keeps a pop-out and the board in step.
- The duplicate paperclip (`#t-attach`) is gone on a phone; the key bar has one. Desktop is unchanged.
- The "fit this screen resizes the terminal for every window" sentence is hidden on a phone and lives in the `#t-view-btn` tooltip.

## Test plan

- On a phone, attach a card: the bar is one row with alias, chevron, full screen. Tap the chevron: chips and buttons appear; tap again: collapsed. Reload: the choice is kept. In full screen and in a popped-out window the same holds and full screen stays one tap away.
- Expanding or collapsing does not raise the keyboard.
- Desktop: no chevron, the bar and paperclip are fully shown.
- Headless: `HEADLESS_ONLY=phoneTermBar,phoneView,u016,phoneFocus`.
