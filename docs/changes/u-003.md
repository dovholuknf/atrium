## @LETTER@. The board washes out a card that is not running

### @LETTER@1. Stopped and live side by side

1. Pin two cards on the board. Leave one running and stop the other.
2. The stopped card is faded and drained of colour, the same look as its row on the Terminals tab.
3. The running card is drawn at full strength. Hovering the stopped card brings it most of the way back.
4. Resume the stopped card. Once its runner is up it is drawn at full strength again.
