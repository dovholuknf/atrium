## Test plan

## @LETTER@. Terminate says terminated only when the session stopped

### @LETTER@1. A done card with a live session

1. Let a card report done while atrium still holds its terminal.
2. Pick terminate and clean up from its terminals menu, and press terminate.

**Expected:** the toast says terminated, its process is gone, and the card leaves the terminals list.

### @LETTER@2. A session that does not stop

1. Terminate a card whose process atrium cannot stop, such as one run elevated by another user.

**Expected:** the toast says could not terminate and names why. It never says terminated.
