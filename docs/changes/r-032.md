## Test plan

## @LETTER@. Keep-alive last/next refresh and today's figures

1. Turn keep-alive on for an idle Claude card on the 1h cache with over 50k context, then `GET` its card view.
   **Expected:** `next_refresh_at` is 5 minutes before `warm_until`; `last_refresh_at` is absent.
2. Wait until that time and let the refresh run.
   **Expected:** `last_refresh_at` is the refresh time and `next_refresh_at` moves 1 hour later.
3. Send the card a new turn, and check a card that is off, stopped, cold or under 50k.
   **Expected:** `last_refresh_at` is gone after the turn; those cards carry no `next_refresh_at`.
4. `GET /v1/settings`.
   **Expected:** `cache_keepalive_today_refreshes` and `cache_keepalive_today_tokens` count only refreshes since local midnight.
