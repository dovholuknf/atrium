## Changelog

- **Lines lost from the middle of a long reply, diagnosed.** See `docs/backlog-2.md` item 74.

  No behaviour change yet. A reply lost 10 rows because the inbox ConPTY (conhost 10.0.26100) answered a change in
  the pty's row count with one bare `\e[H` full-screen repaint, and the rows it had filed into its own history were
  never sent. atrium's ring, replay and the board's xterm.js pass the bytes faithfully. OpenConsole ConPTY 1.24 loses
  nothing under the same resizes. Test-only harnesses under `internal/daemon/conpty_*_repro_test.go` open a pseudo
  console through either one, and are skipped unless their environment variables are set.

## Test plan

## @LETTER@. Rows lost to a ConPTY resize repaint (item 74 repro)

Windows only. Clear `ATRIUM_LOCATION` and `ATRIUM_DEBUG_INPUTLAG` first. The scripts are in
`build.claude/lost-lines/` of the `claude/lost-lines` worktree. Nothing here touches a live room.

### @LETTER@1. Row changes under the inbox ConPTY lose lines

1. Set `ATRIUM_CONPTY_OUT` to a scratch file, `ATRIUM_CONPTY_LOOP=30`, `ATRIUM_CONPTY_RESIZE=flip`,
   `ATRIUM_CONPTY_FLIP_ROWS=40`, and leave `ATRIUM_CONPTY_DLL` unset.
2. `go test ./internal/daemon/ -run '^TestConPTYScrollRepro$' -count=1`
3. `node check.js <out> 120 50 300`, then `node homes.js <out> 120 50`.

**Expected:** numbered lines are missing, and some repaints read `wasAtRow=11` with the height 50 on both sides (the
live signature). The same run's `ATRIUM_CONPTY_DIRECT` control has every line.

### @LETTER@2. OpenConsole ConPTY loses none

1. The same, with `ATRIUM_CONPTY_DLL` naming `conpty.dll` from `Microsoft.Windows.Console.ConPTY` 1.24, with
   `OpenConsole.exe` beside it.

**Expected:** `all present`, with the grid fixed and with `--follow`, and no `\e[H` repaint in the capture.

### @LETTER@3. Same-size resizes and column changes are harmless

1. Repeat @LETTER@1 with `ATRIUM_CONPTY_RESIZE=same`, then with `altc`.

**Expected:** every line present in both.
