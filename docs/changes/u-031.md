## Test plan

## @LETTER@. Shift+right click menu in a terminal

### @LETTER@1. Shift+right click opens the terminal's menu
On the desktop attach a supervised card. Hold Shift and right click on the terminal. The card's terminal menu opens at the pointer with rename, attach and the rest, and "new context" (note "commit, hand off, clear") is on it. The browser's own menu does not show.

### @LETTER@2. A plain right click still pastes
Right click in the terminal with no modifier. The clipboard is pasted as before and no menu opens. Ctrl+right click still gives the browser's menu.

### @LETTER@3. New context from that menu
Choose "new context". The progress chip on the card and tab runs the same steps as Ctrl+Alt+N. While it runs, Shift+right click no longer offers the entry. If it fails the entry returns.

### @LETTER@4. The terminals list row menu
Right click a supervised row on the terminals tab. "new context" is on the menu. On an exited or unsupervised row it is not.

### @LETTER@5. The pop-out and the phone
In a `#term=` pop-out Shift+right click does the same. On a phone a long press still does not paste and opens nothing new.
