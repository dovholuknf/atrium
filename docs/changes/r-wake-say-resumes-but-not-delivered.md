## Test plan

## @LETTER@. A wake say delivers

### @LETTER@1. Wake a parked card with two says

1. Park a card that has a conversation, then say to it twice with wake from another card, a second apart.
2. Watch the card start.

**Expected:** nothing is typed until the session is ready, then one turn carries both messages, and the card shows
running. The card resumed once.

### @LETTER@2. A session that never gets ready

1. Wake a parked card whose runner cannot start.

**Expected:** after three minutes its launcher is told the messages were not taken, and they stay on the card.
