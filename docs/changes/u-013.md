# u-013: the usage tab's limits, the 5h band, and flameout

Design: docs/rnd/usage-tab-design.md sections 3.3, 3.3.1, 3.3.2 (board side), 4.1.

## What changed
- `js/usage-limits.js` (new): `ulFit` (pure: readings in, projection out), the reading store (`UL`), `ulLoad` (first paint fetch), `ulOnCards` (live telemetry, called where `lastTasks` is set in board.js and stack.js), `ulSection`, `ulBand`.
- `js/usage-charts.js`: `ulSection()` appended to the paint, `ulBand` in `ucBurn`, `ulLoad` in `loadUsageTab`.
- `css/files.css`: `.ul*` rules, using the existing tokens and the `ucsplit` bar.
- `index.html`: loads the new file.

## Decisions
- Danger is "the projection lands within 30 minutes of now".
- The flameout mark is on the 5h row only; the week row shows the line without it.
- The token-burn fallback needs the tab's range to reach back to the first reading and no card filter; otherwise it says "no pace to project".
- The room's `card` field is shown as a title if it matches a card id on the board, else as sent.
