## Test plan

## @LETTER@. The phone page at /m

### @LETTER@1. It is served
Open `/m` on the room and on the hub. It lands on `/m/` and the page paints. `/m/manifest.webmanifest` answers as a manifest, the four icons answer as PNG. Add it to the home screen: it opens without browser chrome and starts at `/m/`.

### @LETTER@2. Needs you
Make one card wait on a permission, one ask two questions, one finish a turn nobody has read, one report to you. Open `/m/` on a phone. The four show oldest wait first, each with its reason in words ("wants to run `go test ./...`", "asks 2 questions", "finished 4m ago, not read", "report waiting") and a quiet age. Answer one on the board: its row slides shut with no jump. With nothing waiting the page says "Nothing needs you".

### @LETTER@3. All
Tap "all". Every card shows, grouped working, waiting, idle, with the count on the switch. The choice is remembered after a reload.

### @LETTER@4. The card view
Tap a row. A sheet slides in with the name, status, room and what it is doing. The last three replies show as a conversation with code blocks scrolling inside themselves and no sideways page scroll. A reply cut at 16KB says so. A codex card shows its screen tail labelled "from the screen". The recap and open questions follow. "open terminal" opens `/#term=<id>`. The browser back button closes the sheet.

### @LETTER@5. Replies are safe
Have a card reply with `<script>` text and a `javascript:` link. The text shows as text, the link shows as plain words, an `https://` link works.

### @LETTER@6. Replies refetch on a finished turn only
Watch the network tab with a card open. Nothing polls `/replies`. When the card ends a turn the replies refresh once. On a room without the replies endpoint the recap and the last report show instead.

### @LETTER@7. Live dot, skin, rooms
The dot is green while the stream is up and turns amber when it drops, and the page reconnects on its own. The page wears the skin picked on the board, dark and light. Behind the hub with several rooms the room name shows in the bar and each row carries a room tag.

### @LETTER@8. The board's phone link
On a phone the board's slim header shows "phone view" and it opens `/m/`. On desktop it is not shown.

### @LETTER@9. Headless
`HEADLESS_ONLY=mServe,mHome,mCard,phoneHeader` with Playwright installed, and `go test ./internal/api/ ./internal/link/ -run 'PhonePage|BuildIDCoversThePhone|HubServesThePhonePage'`. Set `M_SHOTS=<dir>` to write the screenshots.
