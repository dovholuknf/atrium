## Test plan

## @LETTER@. The documents page layout

### @LETTER@1. The list of one card

1. On a hub board, hold the pointer on a card that published documents and click "published N documents".

**Expected:** a centred column no wider than about 880px. The header row reads "This card" with the count, a "this card" and "every card" switch, and a search field. Under it, upload sits left with its title field inside it, and "deleted" sits right and quiet. Each row shows the title, then the kind, versions, age and author. The usage is a thin meter at the foot with "N KB of N MB used" under it.

### @LETTER@2. Every document

1. Click "every card".

**Expected:** the header reads "Documents" with no switch, and the list is every document.

### @LETTER@3. The phone

1. Open the same list at 390px wide.

**Expected:** the switch, the search and the upload control each take the full width, "deleted" sits to the right, rows wrap their meta line, and nothing scrolls sideways.
