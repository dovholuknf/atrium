## Test plan

## @LETTER@. The usage tab: a neutral by-card colour, a toggle that says its state, and a cumulative line

By card and by group use one colour that no kind uses (`--usage-bar`, the label colour at about 70%, so every skin reaches it). Their totals follow the cache reads toggle.

### @LETTER@1. By card is not teal
Open the usage tab with cache reads hidden. The by-card tiles' bars are a dim neutral, not the teal of the `cache read` swatch. Press the toggle: the bars keep the neutral colour and the tile totals grow to include cache reads. Change the skin in the gear: the bars follow it.

### @LETTER@2. The toggle says its state
The button reads `cache reads hidden` when not pressed and `cache reads shown` when pressed, with the teal swatch kept. Hover it: the tooltip says which of the two it is. Change the setting from a second tab: the first tab's label flips without a reload.

### @LETTER@3. The cumulative line
Between `tokens by kind` and `tokens by cause` a line climbs from 0 on the left to the range total on the right, and the total matches the by-kind figures. It never dips. Press the toggle: the total changes to count cache reads.

### @LETTER@4. At this pace
On 24h and 7d a dashed line continues from now to the coming midnight, labelled `at this pace: NM by midnight`. Just before midnight it is short and close to the solid end. On 1h and 6h it is absent, and it is absent on any range when nothing was spent in the last hour.
