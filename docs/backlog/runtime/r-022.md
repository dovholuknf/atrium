# r-022. A new-context cycle armed while the card is running fails in seconds (bug)

Status: BUILT 2026-09-29 on `claude/r-022` by @runtime. Reported by the orchestrator (seen twice on @ui). Room-side.

`POST /v1/tasks/<id>/new-context` while the card was RUNNING failed in about 6 seconds with "HANDOFF.ui.md was not
updated by the capture turn". Posted while the card was idle, it worked.

The cause: `ncType` asked only the in-memory activity whether a turn was going. A turn that ends on background work
(a background shell, a Monitor, a subagent) reads idle in the activity while the card's status stays running, and the
next turn starts when that work reports. The capture was typed into that gap, merged into the turn that followed, and
that turn's Stop was taken as the capture turn's end, before the capture had been answered.

The fix: `ncType`, in both its cheap look and its locked quiet check, and the capture-end wait also require the card's
own status not to be `running` (`cardRunning`). The cycle then types only between turns, so the turn count taken
before the capture is the capture's own. The test rig's turn ending (`ncTurnEnds`) now does what a Stop does, idle plus
`needs-input`. Test: `TestACycleArmedWhileRunningWaitsForTheTurnToEnd`.
