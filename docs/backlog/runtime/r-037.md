# r-037. A board freeze, and a token and tool-call budget that trips it (feature)

Status: design only, clint ranks. Owned by @runtime. Filed 2026-09-30 for @rnd from `docs/rnd/competitor-features.md`
(item 1, claude/rd-002 61eb32b5). Design: `docs/rnd/freeze-budget-design.md` (@rnd). Not built.

A way to stop the whole board at once, and a budget that pulls it for you.

- A freeze: every supervised runner stops taking new work (no new prompts, messages or wakes delivered, and
  permission requests answered no, or held) until it is lifted. It is a daemon state shown on the board, and it is
  NOT a kill. A kill is not a stop.
- A budget: token spend and tool calls over a window, board-wide and perhaps per card, that trips the freeze when
  passed. OFF by default.
- Open until the design settles it: where the freeze sits in the permission chain (its order is critical), and
  whether a trip notifies before it freezes.
