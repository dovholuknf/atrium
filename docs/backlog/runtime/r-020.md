# r-020. The hub's auto approvals are recorded as `global-auto`, not as the operator (bug)

Status: BUILT 2026-09-29 on `claude/r-020` by @runtime. Agreed with @fabric (it is the room half of @fabric's f-008)
and approved by the orchestrator. Room-side.

Today the hub approves a room's permission through the room's decide route with only a reason, so the room stores
`decided_by: "you"`: an unattended approval is recorded as the operator's own. `store/review.go` counts `global-auto`
as unattended and `you` as attended, so the review and the ledger are wrong about who decided.

- `POST /v1/permissions/{id}/decide` gains an optional `by`. The room accepts exactly one value from a caller,
  `global-auto`, and answers 400 to any other non-empty value, so no caller can forge a rule's name or another
  decider. Absent means `you`, as today.
- NOT built: the `via: "hub"` mark first proposed. The room cannot tell a request that came through the hub from one
  sent to it directly, so the mark would be a guess. A room's own global auto never comes through this route (it
  answers in `onPermRequest`), so `global-auto` on a decide-route decision already means the hub.
- The hub's `/v1/rooms/{room}/permissions/{id}/decide` forwards `by` (@fabric's half). An old room ignores the field
  and keeps recording `you` until it is updated.

Tests: `by: global-auto` is stored and counted as unattended, a forged `by` answers 400 and decides nothing, no `by`
is `you`.
