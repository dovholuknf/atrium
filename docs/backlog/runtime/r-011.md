# r-011. The reaper assumes a supervised card gone for silence, because a hook zeroed its pid (bug, HIGH)

Status: designed, sent to @rnd 2026-09-29. Approved to build by the orchestrator once @rnd has seen it. Owned by
@runtime. Room-side.

## What happened

On m1mini at 14:56:53 local, room.log said `t-003b ... assumed gone: silent for 15m0s and no pid to check` for card
`01a0ee79`, a SUPERVISED card with a live runner. At 14:57:13 the next tick said `filed dead with a live runner on it,
back on the board`. So the card flapped from running to dead to needs-input in 20 seconds, lost its column, and
wrote two false events.

## Two causes

**1. The reaper asks silence before it asks the supervisor.** `reapOnce` (`internal/daemon/reaper.go`) walks the
running and needs-* cards. For a card with no pid it falls back to `QuietAfter` (15 minutes of silence) and files it
`dead`. It never asks `d.sup.get(t.ID)`, which is the one source that knows for certain: atrium holds the card's
pseudo terminal, and the supervisor drops its entry only in `awaitExit`. `reviveOwnedDead` does ask the supervisor,
one tick later, which is why the card came straight back. The two halves of the reaper disagree about what they
trust.

**2. A report with no pid erases the pid on file.** `refreshObservedAt` (`internal/store/tasks.go`) writes
`pid = obs.PID` unconditionally, and `Observe` keeps worktree, runner, wire name, repo and hostname when a report
leaves them empty, but not the pid. Several callers build an observation with no pid at all: the message and Stop
path (`messages.go`, `observedFor(in.Agent)` with no PID), and the permission hook whenever it does not send one. So
the first such report after the session hook recorded the pid sets it back to 0. "Observed never overwrites with
nothing" (the comment in `Observe`) already holds for every other field. The pid was missed. r-004 fixed the same
shape for the worktree.

## The fix

1. **A supervised runner is alive by definition.** In `reapOnce`, a card with a live entry in the supervisor's
   `runners` map is skipped before either check: no pid check and no silence check. Its liveness is the pty, and its
   exit is filed by `awaitExit`. A card whose runner has gone is not in the map, and falls through to today's checks.
2. **A report with no pid keeps the pid on file.** `refreshObservedAt` and `Observe` treat `obs.PID == 0` as "not
   reported" and keep `t.PID`, as they do for the other observed fields. A new, nonzero pid still replaces it, since
   a restarted session has a new process.
3. **No change to `QuietAfter` or to unsupervised cards.** A window-mode or joined session with no pid still falls
   back to silence, which is the case the rule was written for.

## Tests

- A supervised card, `running`, pid 0, silent for an hour, with a live runner in the map: `reapOnce` leaves it
  `running` and writes no event.
- The same card with a pid that `processAlive` says is gone: still left alone while the runner is in the map.
- The same card once its runner leaves the map: today's behaviour (dead after `QuietAfter`, or on a gone pid).
- An unsupervised card with no pid, silent past `QuietAfter`: still filed dead.
- Store: a report with pid 0 keeps a stored pid. A report with a new nonzero pid replaces it. Both through
  `Register` and `Observe`.
- Daemon: a message or Stop hook for a card whose session hook recorded a pid leaves the pid in place.
