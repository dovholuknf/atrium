# r-035. Lean workers get a narrower gateway than the whole of mercurius (security)

Status: not started. Owned by @runtime. From @rnd's gateway research (`docs/rnd/gateway-fit.md`, finding 5), passed
on by the orchestrator 2026-09-30. Design first, reviewed by @rnd before it is built. After r-029.

`~/.atrium/mcp.json` gives every lean worker `mercurius` at `http://127.0.0.1:8088/mcp`, which is the whole
mcp-gateway. So a worker sees every backend's tools, `discourse_create_user`, `discourse_update_user` and
`discourse_delete_query` included, when it needs the review tools at most.

- Options, cheapest first: a second gateway listener with a narrower config for workers (the listener is the
  identity), or per-backend `tools: allow` lists in that config. Neither is atrium code: the atrium side is which URL
  and name a lean launch writes into its MCP config, and a per-harness or per-launch choice of it.
- The design says what a worker needs from the gateway (mercurius review rounds, and nothing that writes to discourse
  or manages users) and who runs the second listener (atrium never holds the gateway's credentials).
