# r-008. `atrium_say` refuses a unique card-id prefix that the board accepts (bug, LOW)

Status: not started. Backlog only, low priority, do not build yet. Owned by @runtime. Filed 2026-09-29 at the
orchestrator's request.

`atrium_say` to `01a0ee2c-06ee`, the first 13 characters of @merge's card id and unique on the board, answered
`no session called "01a0ee2c-06ee"` with the list of handles that would have worked. The full handle worked. The
board accepts a unique prefix of a card id. The say path does not: it resolves a full card id, a handle, an alias,
`name@room` and `room~id`, and nothing shorter than a full id.

Fix: in the peer resolvers (`localTarget` and `resolvePeer` in `internal/daemon`, the MCP `resolvePeer` in
`internal/link/control_mcp.go` and the CLI copy in `internal/cli/control_peers.go`), after an exact id, handle or
alias misses, accept a card-id prefix that matches exactly one card that would otherwise be a valid target. An
ambiguous prefix answers with the matching cards, never a guess. Keep a minimum length (8 characters) so a two-letter
typo cannot land somewhere. Share one helper so the four resolvers cannot drift, which the done-card alias fix (item
89b) had to patch in three places.

Tests: a unique prefix resolves in each resolver, an ambiguous one lists its matches, and one under the minimum is
refused.
