# t-004. Runners and their pseudo console run at AboveNormal (bug)

Filed 2026-09-29 by @terminal and approved to build by the orchestrator. Owned by @terminal. Worker `t-004` (card
`01a0ee68-a505-7bb8-aed8-585a4211ae51`, branch `claude/t-004`).

Keystrokes lag under load (p95 263ms, max 5.3s, mostly wire time), and clint keeps the machine at 80 to 90% CPU on
purpose. The hiccup probe (`docs/terminal/input-lag-logging.md`) ran at two priorities at the same moment on 2026-09-29 at
14:16. At Normal it logged 571 stalls of 15 to 149ms in 120s, and at AboveNormal it logged zero. The hub and the room
are already AboveNormal. All 29 `claude.exe` runners, the 45 `conhost.exe` and the 6 `OpenConsole.exe` were Normal,
the same class as the load.

CPU at the time, in 10s samples on 20 logical cores: headless chrome 65 to 113% of one core, pwsh about 90%, the
room 72 to 122% (see t-005), claude 56 to 113%, Task Manager about 30%, store.test about 25%.

Fix: the room raises each interactive runner and its conhost or OpenConsole to AboveNormal at spawn, behind a room
setting `runner_priority` (default `above_normal`, or `normal`). Windows passes a priority class down to a child only
for Idle and BelowNormal, so the builds and browsers agents start stay at Normal and keep the machine busy below the
key path. Keep-alive forks, sources and recognisers stay at Normal. Needs a room restart.

Not explained yet: the 5.3s max. The probe's worst stall was 149ms, so a stall that long is something else, and the
lag log around the moment it happened would be needed to find it.
