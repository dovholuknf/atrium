# f-011. Rolling room restart: upgrade atrium without stopping your agents (design)

Status: approved by clint as a design item, 2026-09-29. Filed by @rnd. The design starts after the claude-sg4
restart, and @rnd writes it. @fabric owns the build once @rnd and clint approve the design. Related: f-004 (two rooms
on one machine, to bring one up and migrate) and `docs/room-requirements-design.md` section 5 (scope with two rooms).

## The product feature

**Upgrade atrium without stopping your agents.** A new atrium build lands, and every agent keeps working through it.
Each one moves to the new build at its own next quiet moment, its conversation, directory and name intact. Nobody
has to get every session idle at the same time, and nobody loses a turn to an upgrade. This is for anyone running
atrium, not only for building atrium.

## The problem, as it bit today

A room restart needs every card idle at the same moment. On a busy room that moment rarely comes, so fixes that are
merged and deployed hub-side wait for the room. On 2026-09-29 about twelve room-side fixes sat unlanded that way,
among them r-001, r-007 stage 1b and item 91. They were found and fixed faster than the room could restart.

## The idea

Run a SECOND room on the same machine with the new build. Move cards from the old room (A) to the new one (B) one at
a time, each at the moment THAT card is idle: exit it on A, then resume the same conversation, in the same directory,
on B. When A holds no cards, retire it. Restart-in-place already does exit-then-resume (parking at a restart,
`docs/keepalive-policy-design.md` section 4, and the restart wake). The new part is resuming on a DIFFERENT room.

## Hazards the design must answer

1. **A second room steals the hooks.** Hooks find their room through `ATRIUM_LOCATION` and the location file
   (`daemon.json`). The hooks file (`~/.claude/settings.json`) is shared by every room running as one account. Ports
   and the database are per room. A throwaway second room has taken the hook pointer before (the orchestrator's
   memories `throwaway-room-hijacks-hooks` and `throwaway-atrium2-needs-atrium-location`). The design says how each
   card's hooks reach the room that now holds it, during the move and after it, and how B never captures a card A
   still holds. It also says how A and B keep their own state dirs, ports and databases (`docs/rnd/room-autostart-
   design.md` section 2.4 has the state-dir pin).
2. **Held and queued messages follow the card.** Messages queued for a card on A (`message` rows, held peer
   injections, restart wakes, owed reports) move with it to B, are delivered exactly once, and are never left on A
   or duplicated.
3. **Handles and aliases keep resolving.** `@alias`, `name@room` and `room~id` all still reach the card after it
   moves. That covers atrium_say, atrium_report to a launcher, report_to (r-014), the ledger's launcher links, and a
   parked card's wake (r-007). A sender holding an old `room~id` is either routed or told where the card went.
4. **The new-context cycle hold.** A card mid new-context (capture, `/clear`, wake, r-016) is never moved. The move
   waits for the cycle to finish, and a move never starts a cycle.
5. **A supervised pty moving under an attached browser.** Moving means ending the pty on A and starting a new one on
   B. The design says what a board attached to that terminal sees: a reattach to B's terminal, the replay, the
   pop-out window, the phone viewer (t-003), and the input line of a human who was typing.
6. What "idle" means for the move, reusing r-007's park rule: no turn, no permission, no question, no queued
   message, no background work or subagents, no live worker of its own. Plus how the whole move is started,
   watched, paused and rolled back, and what happens to a card that never goes idle.

## Out of scope until the design says otherwise

Moving a card between machines. This is one machine, two rooms, and the old room retiring.
