# f-029. Notify: `input` gated on a recorded turn end, and the prune body cut silently

Status: open. Owned by @fabric, with a question for @runtime. Found by @review's cr2 correctness review: a MEDIUM
(unproven) and a LOW (`docs/review/cr2-ada747b-2ce6443.md`).

1. **MEDIUM, a question: `input` is silenced for a card with no `turn_ended_at`** (internal/link/notify.go:246 on
   landing).
   - The gate drops `needs-input` while `TurnEndedAt == ""`. Only the Stop hook path (`store.NoteTurnEnded`, through
     `daemon.noteTurnForSeen`) writes that field, and the Stop hook is optional.
   - Since cr2, `&& report == ""` keeps a card that reported. A card that never reports, with no Stop hook or joined
     part way, may still never notify.
   - The question for @runtime: can a card reach `needs-input` and stay there with an empty `turn_ended_at`? If it
     can, gate on the card's age, or on its first prompt, and not on a turn end.
2. **LOW: the prune body is cut at 64 KiB with no error** (internal/link/prune.go:38).
   - The cut JSON is sent to every room, and the caller gets a 502 with a parse error.
   - Fix: read `1<<16 + 1` bytes, and refuse with 413 over the limit.
