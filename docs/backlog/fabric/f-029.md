# f-029. Notify: `input` gated on a recorded turn end, and the prune body cut silently

Status: done on claude/f-029. Owned by @fabric, with a question for @runtime. Found by @review's cr2 correctness review: a MEDIUM
(unproven) and a LOW (`docs/review/cr2-ada747b-2ce6443.md`).

1. **MEDIUM, a question: `input` is silenced for a card with no `turn_ended_at`** (internal/link/notify.go:246 on
   landing).
   - The gate drops `needs-input` while `TurnEndedAt == ""`. Only the Stop hook path (`store.NoteTurnEnded`, through
     `daemon.noteTurnForSeen`) writes that field, and the Stop hook is optional.
   - Since cr2, `&& report == ""` keeps a card that reported. A card that never reports, with no Stop hook or joined
     part way, may still never notify.
   - The question for @runtime: can a card reach `needs-input` and stay there with an empty `turn_ended_at`? If it
     can, gate on the card's age, or on its first prompt, and not on a turn end.
2. **LOW: the prune body is cut at 64 KiB with no error** (internal/link/prune.go:38).
   - The cut JSON is sent to every room, and the caller gets a 502 with a parse error.
   - Fix: read `1<<16 + 1` bytes, and refuse with 413 over the limit.

## Answer (claude/f-029)

Item 1, for @runtime: YES, a card can sit at `needs-input` with an empty `turn_ended_at`, proved from the code.
- The only writer of `turn_seen.turn_ended_at` is `store.NoteTurnEnded`, and its only caller is `noteTurnForSeen`
  (internal/daemon/messages.go:372), on the Stop hook's final branch (no queued message, no subagent out).
- Paths to `needs-input` that skip it: the idle-prompt and `waiting` Notification (`turnEndedBecause`,
  internal/daemon/activity.go:885), SessionStart (`started`, session.go:446), the reaper (`started`, reaper.go:94),
  `atrium_help` (help.go:182), a report (finish.go:183) and launch (launch.go:612).
- A runner with no Stop hook, or a card joined part way, reaches `needs-input` by the idle prompt alone and never
  gets a turn end, so `input` stayed silent for it for good.
- (`turn_end`, a different table written by SetStatus, is not what the gate reads.)

Fix: the gate now stays quiet only for `waiting_reason == "started"` or a card created under five minutes before it
began waiting (`youngAt`, internal/link/notify.go). An unreadable timestamp keeps the old quiet answer. Tests in
notify_test.go cover an old card, a new card and a started card. Item 2: the prune body is read to `1<<16 + 1` and
refused with 413 (prune_test.go).
