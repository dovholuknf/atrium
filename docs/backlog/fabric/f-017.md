# f-017: the hub's notify trigger, and the notify-command sink

Status: DONE, in claude/main (`add59d7d`), live on the hub. Off until turned on.

Wave 1 of phone notifications, from section 6 of `docs/backlog/ui/mobile-design.md` ("Proposal G-cmd"). Web Push is
wave 2 and is a second sink behind the same `Sink` interface.

## What and why

clint wants his phone to buzz when a card needs him once the browser is minimised. Rooms already announce their whole
card list to the hub within two seconds of a change, so the hub is the one place that sees every room. After each
announcement it works out per card why a human is wanted, compares that with what it last acted on, and hands only
the changes to a sink. The sink is a command the operator writes, so atrium holds the name of a command and never a
credential.

## Decisions

- Reasons, by fixed priority: permission, question, input, finished. Identity is card id, reason and the timestamp
  that made it a reason (`waiting_since`, `questions_at`, `turn_ended_at`). Cards tagged `origin:agent` are skipped.
- A room's first announcement ever, and turning the feature on, store identities without running the sink.
- Fire and forget: a bounded queue (32, oldest dropped and counted) and one worker. The announcement ack never waits.
- Presence is edge triggered. A desktop tab POSTs `/_hub/presence` and opens its stream with `tab=<id>`. The hub drops
  a tab when its stream request ends, with a ten minute backstop for a tab it cannot tie to a stream. While any tab is
  visible a changed identity is stored and the sink does not run.
- `notify_sent` rows are kept until their identity changes, not until the card leaves the cache, so a returning card
  does not re-fire. `at` is the last announcement the card was in, and only rows absent for over seven days are pruned.
- The command is an argv run with no shell, ten second timeout with the process killed, output capped while read.
  The card is given in env and on stdin and never in argv. Three failures in a row switch it off, and
  `disabled_reason` is persisted.
- Guests: a lent session is served by a room's guest listener, which has no `/_hub/` and wraps that room's board. The
  hub serves no guest listener, so there was nothing to refuse. If a hub ever serves one, `notifyapi.go` says where.

## Built

- `internal/hubstore/notify.go` and migration `0003_notify_sent`.
- `internal/link/notify.go` (identity, presence, `CommandSink`, the notifier), `notifyapi.go` (`GET` and `PUT
  /_hub/notify`, `POST /_hub/notify/test`, `POST /_hub/presence`), and the `tab` hook in `serveEvents`.
- Wired in `internal/cli/atrium_run.go` through `atrium_notify.go`.
- Tests for identity and priority, the seed, one run per identity, a card leaving and returning, suppression, the
  stream clearing a tab, the backstop, three failures, the timeout kill, the output cap, the queue, PUT validation,
  and that `/v1/tasks/<id>/replies?n=` reaches the owning room with its query intact.
