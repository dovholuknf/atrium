# 49. The orchestrator can appear on every room (design)

Status: @fabric, design reviewed `1ccecf2`, the 3 questions decided by @fabric (below), READY TO BUILD. Group: design.

Raised 2026-09-28 by clint. A card belongs to one room today, so the orchestrator session is reachable and visible
only from its own room's view. clint wants to be able to put it on every room: seen in each room's view, and
addressable from each. Ideate first: what "on every room" means for a card that runs in one place, and what each
room's view shows of it.

**Status, 2026-09-28: deep backlog, not started.** Moved there by clint.

**Status, 2026-09-29: design reviewed, ready to build.** `docs/rnd/everywhere-card-design.md`: a tag
`atrium:everywhere`, a hub index of tagged cards, bare names that miss locally fall through to it for say, tell and
task, and a scoped view that shows those cards with a room chip. Mercurius round 1 was `ready_to_build`, its one
advisory folded in as FE10. The board's part is @ui's.

**Decided by @fabric, 2026-09-30** (clint's standing order: directors answer technical and product questions). The
doc's three questions, each answered with its own recommendation: (1) the tag is `atrium:everywhere`, and @ui hides
its chip the way it hides `atrium:lean`. (2) Answering a foreign card's permission from a scoped view comes later, as
a separate change. (3) The orchestrator is tagged by hand, never by a rule, so there can only ever be the cards
somebody chose. **Ready to build**: the hub index and the bare-name fall-through (fabric), then the scoped view's room
chip (@ui).
