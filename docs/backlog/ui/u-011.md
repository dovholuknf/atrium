# u-011: usage tab, cache reads toggle and the "0 calls" fix (board half)

From the orchestrator, 2026-09-29. Design: `docs/rnd/usage-tab-design.md` at 622fdb9 on claude/rnd, sections 1, 2
and 3.1 (ranks 1 and 2). Standing rule from clint: keep the current look. With the toggle on, the tab is exactly
today's tab.

## What

1. **Cache reads toggle, off by default.** One small button at the right of the range bar, in the range buttons'
   style: `cache reads` with its legend swatch, pressed or not. Stored as the daemon setting `usage_cache_reads`
   (default off) through `internal/api/settings.go`, like `board_skin`. Per board, not per tab. Off means:
   - burn rate stacks the four counted kinds, and the peak and axis are counted tokens per minute
   - by card sorts and totals by counted tokens
   - tokens by kind splits over the four counted kinds, plus one line in the legend's style:
     `cache reads 1451M · not in these charts · 98% of input was served from the cache`
   - tokens by cause shows counted tokens, with cache reads as a dim column of their own
   - the burn-bar hover still lists all five kinds
   - the card details' 24h chart (`paintCardUsageChart`) follows the setting
   One `ucCounted(sums)` beside `ucTokens` holds the rule (in + out + cache write 5m and 1h). u-010 uses it too.
2. **"0 calls" fix, board half.** `ucSums` gains `replies`, summed like the other fields, with a missing value
   treated as 0 until @runtime's store half lands (`UsageSums.replies` in `internal/store/usagebuckets.go`).

## Not in scope

Ranks 3 to 8 wait for clint. The store half of the replies fix is @runtime's.
