# u-032: the cache state on every Claude card, and a line that counts it

clint could not see that keep-alive was working. The chip existed only on the board columns and said "watching".
Now one function, `keepaliveChip` in `js/keepalive.js`, draws one chip per card that carries `t.keepalive`, on the
stack row, the terminals list row, the board card and the attached terminal's header. A card with no `keepalive`
(not Claude) draws nothing. Hub only: it uses the fields the daemon sends today.

## The chip

Times are local HH:MM, with `tomorrow`, `yesterday` or a date when not today. First match wins.

| state | text | short (phone) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `stopped:break-even`, `miss`, `failing`, `acted` | `⊘ stopped · not worth it / cache missed / refresh failing / refresh used a tool` | `⊘ not worth it` |
| `off`, warm_until ahead | `○ off · warm → 03:32` | `○ off` |
| `off`, cold or unknown | `○ off · cold` | `○ off` |
| on, refreshes > 0, why "not due" | `❄ kept warm 3× · next ~03:27` | `❄ 3× next ~03:27` |
| on, warm, why "not due" or empty | `❄ warm → 03:32` | `❄ → 03:32` |
| on, warm, why anything else | `❄ warm → 03:32 · won't refresh: busy` | `❄ → 03:32` |
| on, warm_until past | `❄ cold since 02:04` | `❄ cold` |
| on, no warm_until | `❄ no cache yet` | `❄ no cache yet` |

`next` is shown only when the daemon says the card is simply not due, and carries a tilde because the board infers
it as warm_until minus the 5 minute margin. A card that is busy, small, on the 5 minute cache, has local hooks, has
a dialog open, has spent its budget or is parked says `won't refresh` and its word instead. The raw why and raw
state are in the tooltip. "Off" is state `off` on the wire.

A chip past its time flips with one `setTimeout` for the soonest flip across the drawn cards, re-armed after each
repaint, and again on `visibilitychange`. It makes no request.

## The line

`cache: 5 warm · 2 kept warm · 9 cold · keep-alive 83 refreshes this week` in the stack's summary bar and above the
terminals list, counted over the visible, not archived Claude cards with the chip's own bucket. The week figure is
`cache_keepalive_week_refreshes` from `/v1/settings`, fetched once and cached. Tapping it opens the gear on the
keep-alive setting. The spend today is not derivable from the usage data the board holds without a fetch, so it is
drawn only when the daemon sends `cache_keepalive_today_tokens`.

## Asks for @runtime

- `last_refresh_at` and `next_refresh_at` on the card view. The board uses `next_refresh_at` when present
  (exact, no tilde, shown whatever the why) and infers the margin when not.
- `cache_keepalive_today_tokens` in `/v1/settings`, so the line can say `· 365k tokens today`.
