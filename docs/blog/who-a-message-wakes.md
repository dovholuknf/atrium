# Who a message wakes costs more than what it says

Series: The software company: directors and workers. Status: idea. Audience: people building agent messaging.

**Hook.** The same short message cost about eleven times more to the reviewer than to a director, because of what each re-reads per turn.

**Angle.** A message's cost is the receiver's context, so the sender must choose whether to wake.

**Rests on:** atrium_say fyi and needs kinds, held notices. See `docs/blog/inventory.md`.

## Story beats

1. Measured on one room: half of a day's spend was turns started by other agents.
2. The reviewer re-reads hundreds of thousands of tokens each turn.
3. `fyi` versus `needs`, and notices held on the card.
4. The rule a director now follows: tell the orchestrator only what is new.
5. What a hub inbox would save.
