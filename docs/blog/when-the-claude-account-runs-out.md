# When the Claude account runs out

Series: Rooms, the hub and federation. Status: idea. Audience: people mixing coding agents.

**Hook.** Moving a live card to codex or opencode: the conversation can never carry, and an exhausted account cannot write its own handoff.

**Angle.** Switching runners is a cold start from a brief; the design is about writing that brief before you need it.

**Rests on:** runner switch design, room handoff design. See `docs/blog/inventory.md`.

## Story beats

1. Why a conversation does not move between runners.
2. Capture early, at 90% of the limit.
3. A data-only brief when the account cannot take one more turn.
4. `--back` resumes the original conversation.
5. Moving a card between rooms uses the same brief.
