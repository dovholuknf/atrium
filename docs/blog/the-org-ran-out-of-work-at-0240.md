# The org ran out of work at 02:40

Series: The software company: directors and workers. Status: outline. Audience: anyone handing work to agents overnight.

**Hook.** Four hours, about thirty items and ten thousand lines landed, and then every director sat idle on questions only one person could answer.

**Angle.** The bottleneck of an agent org is the human's decisions, and designing ahead of approval has diminishing returns.

**Rests on:** decision list, held questions, the pause, factory evaluation. See `docs/blog/inventory.md`.

## Story beats

1. The run: 260 commits, zero bad merges, 77 new tests.
2. 02:40: more than forty open questions across directors, 26 on one list.
3. The proposal: a decision budget before sleeping, for reversible, settings-gated defaults.
4. Later: fifty decisions counted as skipped or forgotten, and a numbered list answered by number.
5. Held questions: none sent until the human asks.
