Series: The software company: directors and workers. Status: idea. Audience: people routing work to cheaper models.

**Hook.** A cheaper model's audit cited files well but got the severities wrong, which became the rule: cheap models only where a stronger one checks.

**Angle.** A model's output is a draft until something checks it; route by who verifies, not by price.
