# The interview that deleted a design

Series: The one human. Status: outline. Audience: people writing designs for others.

**Hook.** A reviewed, approved design was thrown out at question 4 of an interview, when it turned out the human pictured something else.

**Angle.** Ask for the human's picture first, as a scenario with real names, before asking about any mechanism.

**Rests on:** hub forge rev 1 and rev 2, interviewer brief, interview mode. See `docs/blog/inventory.md`.

## Story beats

1. Rev 1: mirror every repo on the hub, reviewed and landed.
2. The interview: three questions on details of a picture he did not hold.
3. Question 4: the hub owns main, takes pushes, passes everything else through live.
4. Rev 2 rewritten from his answers, and the build items cut.
5. The interviewer brief that came out of it.
