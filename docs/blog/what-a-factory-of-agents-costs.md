# What a factory of agents costs: $1,202 in 41 hours

Series: The software company: directors and workers. Status: outline. Audience: engineering leads weighing agent orgs.

**Hook.** Directors against flat workers against a human driving one model, and how much of the money went on relaying messages.

**Angle.** Cost per landed item, and where it goes, decides the org shape more than model choice.

**Rests on:** factory-shape study, usage tab, operator-focus token re-eval. See `docs/blog/inventory.md`.

## Story beats

1. The eras: a flat factory of workers and one merger, then resident directors.
2. About the same cost per commit, about 2.3 times the throughput.
3. Relays: most of the spend is agents reading what other agents said.
4. A wake costs what the receiver's context costs: $0.07 for one director, $0.78 for the reviewer.
5. What changed: fyi messages, a context ceiling, the reviewer on the best model.
