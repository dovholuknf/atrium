# The growler: built as a priority, switched off two days later

Series: Cards, not agents. Status: idea. Audience: people designing notifications.

**Hook.** An alert that stayed until you acted on it, and why the operator asked for its job to move into the bell instead.

**Angle.** An alert that never goes away turns into a nag, and a nag gets ignored or turned off.

**Rests on:** persistent growler, bell, reminders ladder. See `docs/blog/inventory.md`.

## Story beats

1. The ask: one row every screen reads, for waiting permissions and questions.
2. The build, with a reminder ladder from one minute to two hours.
3. Two days later: off by default, per browser.
4. Where the job went: the bell keeps every alert, and one reminder a day.
5. Lesson for notifications aimed at one human.
