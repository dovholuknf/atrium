CI runs 12 jobs instead of 18: the quick groups share a job (`go-all` on ubuntu, `go-test` and `go-checks-visual` on
macos, `go-checks-visual` on windows). A `board-visual` failure no longer needs a job-level `continue-on-error`:
`ci.sh` records it and leaves it out of its exit status, so a `go-checks` failure in the same job still reddens the run.
