room's link, as `atrium_backlog` does. `atrium ci` reads a repository's CI through the hub (`runs`, `run`, `log`,
`artifacts`, `artifact`) and writes a log to a file, printing its path. `atrium tell --kind fyi` sends news that asks
for no answer. (o-human-cli-parity)
