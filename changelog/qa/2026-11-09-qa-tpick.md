# qa-tpick: the card-name chip covered the phone tray's "cards" button

On a 390px phone with the tray open, `.term-who` was squeezed to 19px (`flex: 1 1 0`, `min-width: 0`) while its mark
and name stayed wider, so they spilled over `#t-pick` and an svg took its taps.

Fix: `phone.css` gives `.term-who` `min-width: 44px` and `overflow: hidden`. The chip stays interactive. It is now
clipped to its own box, so it cannot cover a neighbour, and the bar wraps to give it room instead of crushing it.

See REPORT-tpick.md.
