The hub hears CI. `atrium run --ci-hook-service ziti-hook` binds that ziti service (and keeps retrying while the controller is down), takes a `workflow_run` notice guarded by the secret `atrium ci-hook secret` mints, records it, and tells the sessions the `ci_hook.routes` rules name, holding what it cannot deliver for a day. `scripts/ci-notify.sh` and `.ps1` carry a notice from a workflow or from a shell.
