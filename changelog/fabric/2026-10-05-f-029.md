A card with no Stop hook now notifies for `input`. The gate that kept a just-started card quiet read a recorded turn end,
which only the Stop hook writes, so a card that reached needs-input by the idle-prompt Notification alone never rang.
It now stays quiet only while it is `started` or was created less than five minutes before it began waiting. A prune
body over 64 KiB is refused with 413 and reaches no room, where it used to be cut and sent on to every room.
