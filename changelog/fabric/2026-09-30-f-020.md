The hub audit log now records every mutating control call with who asked (claimed): launch, exit, cull, restart,
wake-after-restart, an alias set or clear, and a say that wakes a parked session. Refusals are lines too, so a launch
turned away by the cap reads `ctl-launch ... refused: ...` and replaces the old `launch-refused` kind. A cross-room call
is written on the target room. Prompts, briefs and message text are never recorded (f-020).
