A room over ziti or zrok is now named by a certificate the hub issued, as a direct room is, so it can no longer say it is
another room. `atrium rooms token` mints the new join string, which the room enrols with over the overlay. Existing
overlay rooms keep attaching on their old join strings, marked unproven in the rooms list and in a `room-unproven`
audit line, until you run `atrium rooms legacy refuse` (f-022).
