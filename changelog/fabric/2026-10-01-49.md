A card tagged `atrium:everywhere` can be reached by its bare name from every other room: `atrium_say`, `atrium tell` and
`atrium_task` fall through to it when the name is nobody on the caller's own room, `atrium_peers` lists it, and two
rooms with the same name are refused naming both. A scoped board can fetch them from `GET /_hub/everywhere?room=` and
stream their events with `?everywhere=1`. (49)
