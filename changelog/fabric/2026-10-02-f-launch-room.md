- A session can launch on another room from its own room: `atrium_launch` takes `room`, as the hub's tool already did. The worker lands on that room with you as its launcher, so its reports and notices come back to you, the brief is written on that room's disk, and the card comes back as `room~id` with a handle `name@room` that `atrium_say`, `atrium_task` and `atrium_exit` take.
- A room that does not exist is refused with the list of rooms the hub knows. A room that is known and not answering is refused and nothing is held, since a launch that arrives late starts a session nobody is waiting for. A launch that may have started is called unconfirmed and is never retried.
- Needs a room deploy for the tool and the hub's new `launch` relay op, either order. A room with an old hub says the hub is older than launching on another room and to update it.
