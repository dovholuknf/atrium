`atrium room setup --spec room.yaml --plan|--apply` makes a room match a spec, on Windows, Linux or a Mac, with no PowerShell:
the work root, the npm, go, pip and cargo caches, the room's `git_root`, `reviews_root` and `context_handoff_dir`, and the
operator's agent pack. `--plan` reads and cannot write (it is given no way to), `--apply` converges and writes
`~/.atrium/room.lock`, and a parent folder the account cannot examine is exit 13 with the administrator's lines. (f-room-spec)
