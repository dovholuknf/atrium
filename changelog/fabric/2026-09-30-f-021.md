A worker (a card tagged `atrium:subagent` and not `atrium:director`) now sees six control tools instead of eleven: status,
peers, say, report, task and alias. Launch, exit, cull, restart and wake-after-restart are left out of its list, which
also saves it their long descriptions. Directors, the merger and untagged sessions see everything, as does any caller the
hub cannot place. The list is read at session start, so a retag lands at the next start (f-021).
