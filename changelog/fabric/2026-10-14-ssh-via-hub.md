A room can let the hub carry ssh to its own sshd, off unless a person at that machine runs
`atrium room remote-access on`. `atrium ssh-proxy <room>` is the ProxyCommand (`--print-config` prints the block for the
OS it runs on), `atrium ssh config add|rm|show` writes the blocks to `~/.ssh/atrium_config` and adds one `Include` line
after a backup, and the Rooms tab shows an `ssh` badge, a facts row and a copy button. Both ends audit every stream and
never its content. ssh-via-hub.
