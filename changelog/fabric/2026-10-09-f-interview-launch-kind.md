- **`atrium_launch` can start an interviewer, and refuses a directory that is not in git.** `kind: "interview"` writes the interviewer rules into BRIEF.md from the binary ahead of your topic, tags the card `atrium:interview`, runs it on opus (or the worker_policy `interview_model`) and gives it a system prompt where its reply is the question. Atrium never nudges it or calls it stuck for stopping on a question. A `cwd` that is not a git checkout on a `claude/*` branch is refused with the reason unless the call says `scratch: true`.
