# atrium v0.0.4

## Highlights

- Terminal paths are links: each folder in an absolute path opens on its own, and a plain left click opens it.
- A room forwards its loopback ports through the hub at /<room>/preview/<port>/, sandboxed.
- A usage tab with a chart whose hover bubble shows date, time, value and rate, and labels the projected line.
- A room sweeps the folders of ended cards, stashing unsaved work to the hub first, and a disk watch keeps an eye on
  free space.
- Access for ziti and zrok, a hub board guard, and room admission.
- A card's checkouts stay inside its home: the guard refuses ones outside it.

## Also

- **Terminal links:** fast on a room with many cards, answers survive switching back to a card, a failed probe is
  retried once, and every path on a wide row is probed. A right click opens a link menu instead of pasting.
- **Paste:** the paste bar clears when the runner echoes the paste, one paste types one path, and image paste goes to
  the card you right clicked and survives slow reads and two pastes in one second.
- **Open:** an empty runner falls back to the recipe or claude, a repo link picks its runner on its own room, Edit on
  a failed repo card reopens its name step, and a bare github owner/repo launch gets a card home.
- **Board:** group by age puts pinned cards in their age bucket, and computed group headings lose the stray +.
- **Reader:** a Raw button opens a text file in a new tab, and Ctrl-A selects only the file.
- **Walk:** fixes to the walk tab.
- **Hub:** hub.err rotates by move, keeping 5, and inputlag lines are rate limited. Two callers racing for one ziti
  context share it quietly.
- **Tests and CI:** steadier probe speed, merge-room and headless page tests, and the test guard drops the room's
  WORKTREE_ROOT.
