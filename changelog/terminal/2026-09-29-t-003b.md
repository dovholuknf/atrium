- **A phone can watch a card without resizing its terminal.** See t-003b.

  A touch-first device now attaches without sending a resize, so opening a card, rotating the phone or raising its
  keyboard never moves the pty or makes the desktop's terminal repaint. The phone draws the pty's exact grid, opened
  so its width fits the screen. Pinch to zoom, drag to pan. A key bar under the terminal has Esc, the arrows, Tab,
  Enter and ctrl-c for Claude Code's menus. `localStorage["atrium.termphone"]` set to `1` or `0` forces the view.
