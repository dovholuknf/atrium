- **A phone can watch a card without resizing its terminal.** See t-003b.

  A phone now attaches without sending a resize, so opening a card, rotating the phone or raising its keyboard never
  moves the pty or makes the desktop's terminal repaint. The phone draws the pty's exact grid, at about 60 columns
  across in portrait and the whole width in landscape. Pinch to zoom, drag to pan. A key bar has Esc, the arrows,
  Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter and a held ^C. "fit this screen" under the terminal goes back to resizing it.
