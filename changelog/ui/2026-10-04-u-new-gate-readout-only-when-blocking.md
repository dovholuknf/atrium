# The typing gate readout is a short dim line, and speaks up only when something is held

With the readout switched on, the line under a terminal now says "line empty" or "203 chars on the line" in dim text when nothing is waiting, and never copies the text you are typing. When a message is held behind your line it says so plainly, for example "1 message from @runtime waits: 203 chars on your line". If the line only looks empty, because atrium counts blanks you cannot see, it quotes what atrium thinks is there, cut short.
