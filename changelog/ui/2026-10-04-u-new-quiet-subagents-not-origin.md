# Quiet the subagents, not every card an agent launched

The "don't notify me" setting now silences only cards tagged `atrium:subagent`, the workers. A director or the orchestrator that an agent launched notifies again, with its toast, sound and desktop notification. A permission request from a subagent still notifies, and so does a card with a tone of its own. The terminals "subagents" hide toggle now means the same cards.
