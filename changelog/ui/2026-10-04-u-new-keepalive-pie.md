# The cache chip is a pie that fills toward the next refresh

The small cache mark on a card is now a pie. For a warm card with a refresh coming, the wedge fills from the last refresh (or from when the cache was written) toward the next refresh, and turns amber when it is nearly full. A stopped card is an empty circle struck through, a cold card is a solid blue-grey dot, and the tooltip carries the exact times and the reason. It repaints once a minute from the browser clock, with no extra requests and no animation.
