# The walk drawer gives the finding more room on a phone

On a phone the walk drawer was 62% of a short pane, so the finding got about 130px and its buttons sat a scroll away with
none of the finding beside them. The drawer now takes up to 80% of the pane, which leaves about 190px for the finding.
At 360px wide the walk head also wrapped badly once a finding was marked, pushing walk done and the close button off the
right edge. The head now wraps and the count takes its own line.
