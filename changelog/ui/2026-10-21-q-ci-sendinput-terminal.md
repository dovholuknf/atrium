Fixes the terminal invariant check that failed every board job in CI. The new-context gate put its check ahead of the scroll in `sendInput`, so a paste returned before the view went to the bottom and the check no longer found the scroll. `sendInput` now scrolls and starts the follow window for any non-quiet input, typed or pasted, before it is held or sent, and a dropped key still does not scroll. Item q-ci-sendinput-terminal.
