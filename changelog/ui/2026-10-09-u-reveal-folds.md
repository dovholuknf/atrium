Going to a card now opens every collapsed group it sits in: its board column and status group, its project group, the stack's group, the headings above it in the terminals list, the pinned bucket and the row of the card that launched it. Then it scrolls the row into view. It works from the ctrl-k switcher, a notification or toast, a card link and the peek, and what it opened stays open after a reload like any manual expand.
