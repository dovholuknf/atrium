# Quick replies come from one place

The board's growler, the phone board's growler and the phone's growler and compose box now draw their one-press answers with the same code, so a choice has the same words and the same look everywhere. An ask that offers `{choices}` gets a button for each option and a `stop` at the end, and a press sends the option's full words. On a phone a long option is cut to 28 characters with `...` and its full text is the button's tooltip. The `{choices}` markers never show.

The phone compose box now shows quick replies above the box when the card carries them, and leaves what you were typing alone when you press one. A card the room marks as an open question shows no buttons and puts the cursor in the box. The room does not send those fields yet, so until it does only an ask's own `{choices}` draws buttons.
