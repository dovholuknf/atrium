The terminals tab right-click menu is cut to what you use: restart, resume (stopped cards), new context, add to group, remove from group, settings, stop, and terminate and clean up. Alias and rename are one click on the card's title, and the grey sub-lines are gone. Nothing asks "are you sure?" except terminate and clean up when the folder holds work that exists nowhere else, with a "never show me this again" tick. A "show stopped" pill brings stopped cards back into the list, and a long press on a phone opens the same menu. (card-menu)
