# The board idles lighter

The sound button's pulse, shown while the browser has not yet let the page play sound, now pulses three times and then holds still until your first click. It used to loop and kept the screen redrawing at 60 frames a second. The sticky header is now a solid colour from the skin instead of a blurred see-through one. With no terminal open the board's GPU use drops to about a quarter and the redraw rate to about 8 frames a second, and the header looks the same on every skin.
