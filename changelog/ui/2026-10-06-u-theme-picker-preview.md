"Try a theme" keeps a colour for this card or for every card in its repo, and the panel is down to what you act on: the
first option names what the card falls back to, then `this card`, `every card in <repo>` and `cancel`.
An attached card no longer changes colour. A card nobody coloured keeps the skin's look attached or not, and its terminal
takes a neutral palette from the skin (or the stored default) instead of atrium green. A coloured row is worn whether
attached or not by default, so selecting it does not repaint it.
The terminal themes editor, the card colours settings and the picker panel share one preview: the board's own card row in
the theme over a larger terminal sample, updating as colours change.
