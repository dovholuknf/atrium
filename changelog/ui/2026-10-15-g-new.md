- `atrium new <name>` and the launch dialog's `repo` button start new work: the room fetches origin, makes a worktree
  and branch named for the work (off `--from`, the remote's branch of that name, or its default) and starts a card in it.
- With no providers, `repo` says so inside the launch dialog with a button to runners, providers, and no second modal
  opens over it. A failed browse or repo pick is a line in its own dialog (g-new-from-checkout, g-new-dialog).
