- Hover the "messages waiting, clear the line" notice and choose "Let it through". Your typed line is copied to the
  clipboard, cleared, the waiting messages are delivered, and the line is typed back without Enter (a newline in it
  as shift-enter). Keys pressed meanwhile are held until it is done. If atrium cannot read the line exactly it only
  copies it and says "your text is on the clipboard" (u-013).
