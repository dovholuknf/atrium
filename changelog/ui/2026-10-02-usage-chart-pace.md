The usage tab's cumulative tokens chart is zoomed to the 5h window on the day ranges and gains the even-pace diagonal (100% exactly at the reset, so you can see whether the window is being spent faster than it lasts), a rate-per-bucket strip on the same time axis, and a crosshair that follows the pointer or a finger: a vertical line, a dot on the curve, a tag riding it, and a line with the time, tokens counted, percent of the limit, rate that hour and how far ahead or behind the even pace you are. On the phone the same numbers are a headline strip above the chart that a finger never covers; a vertical swipe still scrolls the page. Without a card's limit reading (or on a 1h range, with a card filter, or with cache reads shown) the chart keeps plain tokens, now with labelled round ticks, and no percent or pace. The percent is read against the tokens atrium counted, and the chart says so.
