# A new card reaches the terminals list when it is created

A launched card now tells the board the moment it exists, instead of after the runner has settled, and the board reads the
list for a card it has never heard of at once instead of waiting out the five second throttle. The alert and the terminals
row both come from that list, so the gap between "is on the board" and the row is gone.

Until its runner is up the row wears a "starting" chip, not "joined". The room marks a card it is still launching, and the
chip goes when the launch ends.
