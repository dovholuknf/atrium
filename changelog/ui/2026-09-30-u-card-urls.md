A card has an address made of names. `/alias/<alias>` and `/room/<room>/<name>` open its terminal, `/room/<room>` opens the
board scoped to that room, and `/m/alias/...` and `/m/room/...` open it on the phone.
An alias two rooms hold asks which one, and remembers the answer for that address. A bookmark that now opens a different
card says so. Pop-out links and the switcher write the readable path, and old `#term=` links still work. u-card-urls.
