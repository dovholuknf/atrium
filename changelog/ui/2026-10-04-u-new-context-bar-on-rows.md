# The context bar on a row goes green, yellow, red by how full the limit is

The thin line along the bottom of each terminals row (and board card) now changes colour by the share of its limit the card has used: green under 60%, yellow from 60%, red from 85%. At or past the limit the line is full red and pulses once, as before. Hover it for "136k of 200k, window 1M". The limit is still the land-the-plane line. A limit set per runner is not in yet.
