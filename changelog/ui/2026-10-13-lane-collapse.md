- Board tab: folding a group now folds it in that lane only. It used to fold the same group in every column. Added
  "collapse all" and "expand all" to the board's header strip to fold or open every group in every lane at once. A
  group you had folded before is still folded, in every lane, until you open it lane by lane.
- Stack tab: the "pinned" and "the rest" sections now fold like the other groups, and stay folded across a reload.
