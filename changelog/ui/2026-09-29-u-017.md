- Fixed: on a phone, tapping the terminal to type showed the top of the page and bounced up and down. xterm's
  hidden helper textarea was pinned to the grid's top-left, so the browser's focus scroll dragged the pane to the
  top and the cursor keep scrolled it back. The textarea now sits at the cursor on a phone (`terminal.css`, the
  desktop pin is unchanged), and is moved there synchronously on every write.
- The cursor keep only acts when the cursor, the pane box or the visual viewport changed, so it cannot answer its
  own scroll. A focus of the terminal forces one keep. The page is held at scroll 0 in the terminal view.
- The pop-out window (`solo.js`) and the desktop terminal are untouched.
