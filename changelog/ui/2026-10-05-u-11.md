# Clicking `? N` dismisses the questions

Clicking the `? N` chip on a terminal row, a stack row or a board card now dismisses that card's open questions. The click no longer selects the row or opens the card. Enter and Space do the same from the keyboard. A toast says nothing was sent to the session, and the chip comes off at the next repaint. If newer questions arrived since the chip was drawn, nothing is dismissed and a toast says so. An agent reading `atrium_task` sees `answered_via: "dismissed"`. Clicking `!` or `✉` also stops selecting the row and does nothing else.
