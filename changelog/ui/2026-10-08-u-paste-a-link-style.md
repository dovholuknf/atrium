The paste-a-link box (Ctrl+Alt+R) is a command palette: a link icon and a large field with a focus ring, one line of
help instead of two, and keys drawn as keys. As the link is typed or pasted the line reads what it was recognised as
(PR, issue, ticket, branch or plain link), its label and its title, using the recognisers that were already there, and
says why when it is not recognised. Every skin draws it, from the palette tokens. The phone view has no such box.
