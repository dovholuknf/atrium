# A grey row that survives `hide inactive agents` now says why

With `hide inactive: agents` on, a grey (exited) row can still be listed: the card open in the pane is never hidden, and a click on a grey row to start it again makes it the open one. That row now says in its tooltip that hide inactive would hide it but it is open in the pane, so it stays until you switch away. Every other exited row, pinned or not, is hidden as before. The `? 2` chip on an exited card no longer says it clears when you reply, since nothing can reply to it. It says to click to dismiss.
