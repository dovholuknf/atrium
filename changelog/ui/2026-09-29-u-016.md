- The terminal bar and the phone key bar have an attach button (paperclip) that opens the device's file picker, with no `accept` filter, and sends the choice through `uploadIntoTerm`. Hidden for a guest link.
- New full screen button on the terminal bar. One `term-full` class on body hides the header, the session list and the footer strip, and the pane fills the window. On a phone it also asks the browser for real fullscreen, and leaving that leaves the mode. In memory only; detaching leaves it.
- Esc: on desktop it leaves full screen only when the terminal does not have focus, so the runner keeps its Esc. On a phone the key bar's Esc always reaches the runner, and a second Esc within 500ms leaves full screen instead.
- On a touch device the header can be hidden with a small button and brought back from a strip at the top. The choice is saved per device (`atrium.phone.headerHidden`) and survives rotation and reload. With nothing saved, a phone in landscape collapses the header on its own; a saved choice wins.
- Long press on a phone: the key bar's held ^C uses pointer capture, cancels on a drag past 10px or a pointercancel, fires once at 600ms, and the bar turns off the browser callout, selection and touch handling. A long press on the terminal grid no longer pastes the clipboard on a coarse pointer (desktop right click still does).
