# The walk drawer shows removed lines, reads past the hunk, and has a terminal sheet on the phone

The code block in the walk drawer now draws the lines a hunk removes, in a red tint with a `-` and no line number, so a
change reads as a change. Nothing anchors to a removed line.

When the run folder has the head tree (`src/<path>`), `⋯ N more above` and `⋯ N more below` keep growing past the hunk to
the top and bottom of the file, and the count says how much of the file is left. Without `src/` they stop at the hunk as
before.

On a phone the drawer now has the whole pane, with the rail as a strip under the finding, and the terminal is a sheet
pulled up from the bottom by a handle. Asking the walker (`a`) brings the sheet up. Closing the drawer puts the terminal
back.
