- **A report or a say can be an fyi, and a card shows how many held notices it has not read.** `atrium_report` and
  `atrium_say` take `kind`: `fyi` for news the receiver need not act on, `needs` (the default, and what any other word
  means) for anything else. An fyi to a card tagged `atrium:orchestrator` or `atrium:hold-notices` is held on that card
  like an automatic notice and never typed or queued, and `atrium_task` `notices: true` reads it back marked `fyi` with
  the sender. It still counts as the worker's report. A `blocked` or `question` report is always `needs`, and so is a
  say that asks for a reply. The holding card's row carries `held_notices` and `oldest_held_at`, cleared when the card
  reads its own notices. A card stuck past the second step of the escalation backoff types one notice to a launcher that
  holds its notices, so a stuck worker is never only a held line.
