- **A report or a say can be an fyi, and a card shows how many held notices it has not read.** `atrium_report` and
  `atrium_say` take `kind`: `fyi` for news the receiver need not act on, `needs` (the default, and what any other word
  means) for anything else. An fyi to a card tagged `atrium:orchestrator` or `atrium:hold-notices` is held on that card
  like an automatic notice and never typed or queued, and `atrium_task` `notices: true` reads it back marked `fyi` with
  the sender. It still counts as the worker's report. Only a `progress` report with no `ask` can be an fyi: `done`,
  `blocked` and `question` reports and any report carrying an ask are `needs`, and so is a say that asks for a reply.
  The holding card's row carries `held_notices` and `oldest_held_at`, cleared when the card reads its own notices. The
  read is stamped with the newest notice it returned and never the clock, so one held between the read and the stamp
  stays unread. A silent stop of ten minutes, or a tool call stuck past the long-tool threshold for two more, types
  one notice to a launcher that holds its notices, so a stuck worker is never only a held line.
