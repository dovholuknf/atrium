- **Growlers for a report and for a stalled deploy hold.** A card whose session reported `blocked` or `question` with
  `atrium_report` raises a growler at once, with the ask as its body, and it ends when the card runs again. A worker
  tagged `origin:agent` raises none, because its launcher is told. A room deploy hold still on after fifteen minutes
  (`growl.hold_after`) raises a `deploy-hold` growler naming who held it and why, with the hold id as its subject, and
  it ends when the room lifts the hold. Both read what the room already publishes, so this is hub side only.
