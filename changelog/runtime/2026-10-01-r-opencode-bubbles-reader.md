The room runs `opencode export <session id>` (no shell, a timeout, a stdout cap, the id checked against `^ses_[A-Za-z0-9]+$`, one export in flight per card, its answer kept for 3 seconds) and never reads opencode's database or auth.json. Any failure, or no opencode on PATH, answers from the screen as before.
