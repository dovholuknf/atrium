- **A room can say what it can run.** `POST /v1/preflight` on the board listener checks tools, runner sign-in and
  environment names by fixed key from inside the room process, and reports its pid and `--started-by`. (r-018)
- **`atrium requirements <file> --json`** parses an `atrium.requirements.yaml` and prints it normalized, refusing an
  unknown key, an absolute path, or anything that looks like a secret. (r-018)
- **`--started-by <kind>:<nonce>`** on `atrium room` and `atrium daemon`, kept in memory only. (r-018)
