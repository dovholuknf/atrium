- **A worker that stops silently is nudged to continue, twice, before its launcher hears.** (Runtime, deploy to take effect.) Atrium types one continuation line into a launched worker whose turn ended with no done, blocked or report, and a second one if it stops silently again. Only a third silent stop under the same launcher prompt sends the launcher the silent-stop notice. A report, or a new prompt from the launcher, starts the count again. Interviews, residents, directors and cards waiting on a permission are never nudged. Item r-silent-stop-nudge.
