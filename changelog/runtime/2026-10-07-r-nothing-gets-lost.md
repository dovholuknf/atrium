A say to a parked, done or dead card is now kept on that card and delivered the next time it runs, instead of refused (r-nothing-gets-lost).
`wake` now resumes a done or dead card that has a conversation, as it already did for a parked one.
A relayed say that is given up on after a day comes back to the sender as a message with its words. The board's task rows carry `messages_undelivered`.
