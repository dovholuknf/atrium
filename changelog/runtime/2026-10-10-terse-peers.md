Peer messages are short. Every one arrives as `@sender says: "text"`, a hook delivery adds one line saying a session is
not the human and to answer with an atrium tool and write nothing else. A launcher's done or blocked report is
`@worker done <shortsha>: <recap first line>` or `@worker blocked: <reason>`, with no doubled SHA, card id or path.
The atrium_say, atrium_done and atrium_blocked descriptions ask for one or two sentences and no prose after the call.
