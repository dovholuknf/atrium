- **Room stats now report how late the room's timers fire, and the rooms board draws it as a `lag` row.** (Rooms need a restart to report it.) `GET /v1/room/stats` and the `room-stats` event gain a `latency` section: `drift_p99_ms` is the p99 lateness of a 20ms timer over the last ten seconds, and `drift_series_ms` is the per-minute average of that on the same 60 minute grid as the CPU and memory series. A room that is slow to get to work shows a higher figure, which is what a person feels as keystroke lag. The board shows it beside CPU and memory and turns it red over 50ms. Item r-new-room-response-time.
