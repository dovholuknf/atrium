- **Growlers on the hub: what waits on a human, one row every screen reads.** The hub keeps a `growl` table and raises
  a row for a permission that has waited two minutes (`growl.perm_after`), a turn that ended on Open Questions, and a
  room whose store halted. It resolves the row when the reason ends and keeps it open while a room is offline.
  `GET /_hub/growls` answers the open and snoozed set, `POST /_hub/growls/{id}` dismisses, undismisses, snoozes or
  marks one acted and answers 409 for one already handled, and the hub stream says the whole set as a `growls` event on
  every change and on each reminder (1, 2, 5, 10, 30, 60 and 120 minutes). Reminders go to the notify command too while
  no desktop tab is visible. Hub side only, and nothing on the board draws it until U1. (u-new-persistent-growler R1)
