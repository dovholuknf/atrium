- **The published board's login is harder to get around.** While a public zrok share runs, a change that would turn the login off or leave it unable to let anyone in is refused with a line saying to stop the share first. `PUT /v1/auth` now answers only the operator on the machine (`edge.LocalOperator`), so someone signed in to the published board cannot rewrite the login. A source that gets the password wrong five times waits (2s, doubling, up to 15 minutes, answered 429 before any hashing), and at most two password checks run at once (a third gets 503), so parallel anonymous requests cannot spend memory. Sessions carry a generation that a password, name, allowlist or provider change bumps, and the guard re-checks the subject against the current allowlist, so old cookies stop working at once. Logout is now a POST, and after a password-login logout the browser's cached credentials are asked for again instead of signing straight back in. Room side: live at the next room restart, and everyone signed in is signed out once when it lands. (r-new-sec-auth-guard)
