- **Every claude card starts with `--autocompact` at its atrium limit plus 10 percent.** One function (`autocompactK`, built on `cardLimit`, which `autoThreshold` also starts from) gives the window: `auto_new_context_k`, or `context_ceiling_k` for a director, plus 10 percent, clamped to claude's 100k to 1M, with the setting's default (300k, so 330k) when the mode is off. Applied at launch, resume and restart through a new runner-row field `autocompact_args` (claude seeded `["--autocompact","{autocompact}"]`, migration 0080), and shown in the card details. Atrium cycles at the limit and claude compacts at the backstop. A setting change reaches a card at its next launch or resume. Item r-autocompact.
- **`--autocompact` follows what the runner and the model can take.** The runner is asked once at daemon start (`--help` names the flag, cached): an older claude is started without it, with a log line and "runner does not take autocompact" in the card details. The window is also clamped to the model's when it is known (`[1m]` in the model id is 1M, any other named model 200k), and left to the 100k to 1M range when no model was named. Item r-autocompact.
