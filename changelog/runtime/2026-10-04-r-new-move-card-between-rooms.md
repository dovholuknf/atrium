- **A room now has the steps a card needs to move to another room, and a card that moved is followed.** (Room side only, no deploy, the hub verb is not built yet.) A card can be frozen: terminal input is refused, and says, reports, notices and wakes for it wait in a queue with their ids, under a lease that undoes the freeze by itself if the hub never comes back. The room routes under `/v1/move/` are `freeze`, `renew`, `check`, `capture`, `bundle`, `park`, `moved`, `queue`, `ack`, `release` and `undo` on the old room, and `receive`, `deliver` and `adopt` on the new one, each safe to repeat by move id. `receive` writes only through `safepath` and a name allowlist. `/v1/launch` takes `moved_from`, `pinned` and `pin_order`, and a repeat for one `moved_from` returns the same card. A card with `moved_to` forwards says to where it went and answers `forwarded: moved to B~<id>`, its handle stays reserved, and a worker whose launcher moved reports to the new card. A worktree under `<repo>-worktrees/<name>` is labelled `<repo>`. Migration 0084. Item r-new-move-card-between-rooms.
