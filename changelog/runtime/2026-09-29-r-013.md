r-013: "task" events are coalesced per card. PublishTask now marks the card and returns; one send per card per 200 ms reads the row at send time, so a burst of publishes costs one card's three queries and the board still gets the latest state.
A card deleted before the send sends nothing (its task-removed is the event), an unreadable or halted store sends nothing quietly, and pending sends stop with the server.
