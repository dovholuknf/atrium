- **An asked exit is a column on the card, and terminate and a typed /exit count too.** The record that keeps a card
  or a fixture down across a restart was an event, which `event_cold_kinds` or a compaction by kind could route away,
  and then every exited card would come back. It is now `task.exit_asked_at` (migration 0076, carried over from the
  events), set by an exit from the board or `atrium_exit`, a cull, a terminate, and `/exit` typed in a supervised
  card's own terminal outside a wind-down. A launch clears it. `/resume` answers a refused start as 409 or 400, never
  500. Room side. (r-new-review-ff747683)
