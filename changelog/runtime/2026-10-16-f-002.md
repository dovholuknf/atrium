A launch whose answer was lost no longer duplicates when its retry races a late card. `atrium_launch` (the hub's and the stdio one) makes a random launch key on the first attempt and sends it again on the retry for the same directory, and the room's `/v1/launch` takes it as the optional `launch_key` and returns the card already filed under it, marked `launch_replay`, without starting a second session. A caller with no key, or a room older than the field, keeps the snapshot recovery. (f-002)
