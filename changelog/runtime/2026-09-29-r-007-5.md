- **A card idle for two hours is parked.** The reaper tick parks an `origin:agent` card (or one tagged
  `atrium:park-idle`, or the `atrium:orchestrator` card once nothing else is live) at its prompt with no pending
  permission, question, message or wake, no live worker, and no background work or subagents as its last Stop
  reported, after `idle_park_after` (default 2 hours, floor 30 minutes, `off`, a setting beside the keep-alive ones).
  Idle since is the latest of turn end, prompt and human touch, and the handoff turn does not move it. A director, the
  orchestrator or an opted-in card first writes its `HANDOFF.<name>.md` at 50 minutes idle (or `idle_park_after` minus
  70 minutes, whichever is later), while the cache is warm. A capture that times out parks anyway and says so on the
  card. The card keeps its status and resume id. Waking it queues "read your handoff file" ahead of the waking
  message. (r-007)
- **A say from a parked card's own family wakes it.** A say from the card's launcher, or from one of its own workers
  (`work_item.launcher_id`, which a `report_to` child also rides), resumes a parked card as if `wake=true`, like a
  report does. Every other peer still gets `parked` with nothing queued, and the answer now reads "this card is parked,
  send again with wake=true to resume it". (r-007)
- **Keep-alive leaves a parked card alone.** A parked card has no process and its cache is meant to go cold, so the
  refresh is skipped with the reason "parked". (r-007)
