- **A card idle for two hours is parked.** The reaper tick parks an `origin:agent` card (or one tagged
  `atrium:park-idle`, or the `atrium:orchestrator` card once nothing else is live) at its prompt with no pending
  permission, question, message or wake, and no live worker, after `idle_park_after` (default 2 hours, floor 30
  minutes, `off`, a setting beside the keep-alive ones). Idle since is the latest of turn end, prompt and human touch,
  and the handoff turn does not move it. A director, the orchestrator or an opted-in card first writes its
  `HANDOFF.<name>.md` at 50 minutes idle (or `idle_park_after` minus 70 minutes, whichever is later), while the cache
  is warm. A capture that times out parks anyway and says so on the card. The card keeps its status and resume id.
  Waking it queues "read your handoff file" ahead of the waking message. (r-007)
