A landed worker is pruned minutes after its runner exits, not a day. New setting `landed_reclaim_grace` (seconds, `off`,
default 300) starts when the runner exits. Cards launched over /v1/launch with lifetime work or item count whoever launched
them. A `node_modules` junction or symlink in the worktree is removed as a link and never followed. Each prune, and each
keep (uncommitted changes, unmerged commits), is one line on the card and in the room log (f-prune-merged-worker-worktree).
