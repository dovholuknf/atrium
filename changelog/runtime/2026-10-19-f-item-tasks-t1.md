- An item can have tasks: throwaway scratch workers the owner adds with `atrium_item task_add` (and lists, holds, releases, cancels, retries) that run one at a time on a room in a folder of their own. A worker leaves the files the task named and ends with `atrium_done` (new optional `why`). The room sends the files to the hub's drop and removes the folder only after the hub has them, and the item's Tasks list on the board reads the results. f-item-tasks-t1.
