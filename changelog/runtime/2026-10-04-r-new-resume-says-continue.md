A deploy hold records which held cards were mid-turn when it was set (`running` or `needs-permission`), and adds a card whose gated call it refused. The startup lift types its wake line, now ending "check `git status` first.", only into those cards, and writes a `resume-continue` event on each. A card that was waiting for a human gets nothing and has its held messages released. The existing `unexpected_exit` setting turns this off along with the plain-restart notice. `deploy-batch.ps1` sets the hold through `POST /v1/hold` before the restart and waits for the room to go quiet, up to five minutes. `-NoHold` skips it and logs that only a card mid-turn at the wind-down will be woken. The orchestrator's "commit and wait" say before a deploy is no longer needed, and a card told to wait that way is idle at the hold and not woken. Item r-new-resume-says-continue.
