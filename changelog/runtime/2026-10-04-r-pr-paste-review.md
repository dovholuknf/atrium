- **Pasting a pull request link and launching now starts its review, and the launched card walks it.** (Daemon and board, no deploy.) When the launch dialog made a PR worktree, the launch also sends `POST /v1/prs` to the placed room, then starts the card tagged `pr` and `pr:org/repo#n` and sets it as the row's walker, so the walk drawer shows the findings on that card once the row is ready. A review that will not start is shown in the dialog's note and the card still launches. A second paste of a PR that already has a live walker card attaches that card with a toast instead of starting another. A daemon start now puts reviews cut by the restart (`fetching` or `running`) back to `queued` and starts them, and the runner reuses the steps their folder holds. Item r-pr-paste-review.
