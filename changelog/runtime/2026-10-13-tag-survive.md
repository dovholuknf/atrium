An interview card keeps `atrium:interview` (and every other tag) when it is relaunched, resumed, restarted or unleaned:
a launch onto a card now adds to the card's current tags instead of replacing them. A close and the terminal's
restart-with-full-setup read the card's tags at write time. A human editing tags on the board still removes them.
An interview launch is never tagged atrium:subagent, so it files in the interview group and does not count against the launch cap.
