- `atrium_say` and `POST /v1/tasks/{id}/message` take `force: true`. It types and submits now, past the typing gate and
  the turn rule, for the case where the gate cannot read the line (after a room restart it says "not sure what is on the
  line" and holds every say). A dialog on the terminal, a new-context cycle or a deploy hold refuses it with the reason
  and nothing is queued. The card's history records it as `typed anyway, past the typing gate`, with `forced: true`. This
  room only (f-say-force).
- The held messages chip gains "Type anyway" beside "Let it through": it types what is waiting without touching the
  line, for the case where Let it through refuses because the line is not exact.
- `force` is the operator's, or the target's launcher's. Any other session is refused, and a launcher's force types only
  past a line atrium cannot read, never past a known part written line or a key pressed in the last two seconds.
