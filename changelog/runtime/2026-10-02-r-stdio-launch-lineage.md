- **A worker launched through the stdio `atrium control` `atrium_launch` now has a launcher.** That path posted `/v1/launch` with no `spawned_by` and no `origin:agent` tag, so the room filed every such worker's launcher as `@human` (the board's own dialog): `atrium_report` and its notices reached nobody, owed items had no launcher to keep them, and the worker was told "nobody launched you". It now sends what the hub's `atrium_launch` sends for the parts that are the room's: `spawned_by` from `ATRIUM_AGENT_NAME` (left out, not blank, when the session has no name), the `origin:agent` tag, the `atrium:subagent` tag unless the caller's tags already carry `atrium:director` or `atrium:subagent`, and the report line ending a non-empty prompt. The tags and the line come from one helper in `internal/link` (`AgentLaunchTags`, `WithReportLine`) that the hub uses too. The hub's launch cap is still the hub's. **This changes the lineage of every future stdio launch**: workers launched this way are now agent-launched workers (cullable, owing reports to their launcher), where before they looked like the operator's own. Cards already launched keep the lineage they have. Item r-stdio-launch-lineage.
