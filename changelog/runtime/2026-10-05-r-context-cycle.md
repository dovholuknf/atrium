- **Atrium cycles a card's context at its limit: it asks for a handoff, waits for `atrium ready`, then clears and wakes the card.** (Runtime and board, deploy to take effect; the hub too on a hub board.) There is one trigger: the card passes its context limit. The limit is a hub setting per harness, `claude=200` by default, set in the gear as `context limit per harness` and handed to every room, with a per-card limit and an off switch in the card's details. Past the limit atrium types the limit prompt as soon as the input line is free, even mid-turn: wrap up, write the handoff to `$TEMP/atrium/handoffs/<card id>.md` (a room setting can move it), then run `atrium ready` (named by its full path). It types it again at most once per turn and never clears without the ack. `atrium ready` is a new command: it reads the handoff into the card's history and answers "End your turn now"; at the turn end atrium types `/clear`, then `read <path> and continue.` Every supervised card is covered, a person's own too. The context nudge, the launcher's context notice, the `atrium:subagent` exclusion, the context tags, the auto new context settings and the runner's limit field are gone; old tags and settings are ignored. Item r-context-cycle.
